Atölye Yayın Aracı

Atölye Yayın Aracı is a small publishing tool belonging to a single owner. It automatically uploads original, AI-assisted short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (Gaphex Office). Every upload happens under the owner's own OAuth consent, and AI-assisted content is labeled as such.

This app uses YouTube API Services. Terms: YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
